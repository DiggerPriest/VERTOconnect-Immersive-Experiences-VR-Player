# Mining reference review — 2 November 2026

This is a visual-reference review, not an engineered mine design or safety certification.

## Sources checked

- Epiroc ventilation systems: https://www.epiroc.com/en-ro/applications/mining/underground-mining/ventilation — complete fan stations and PVC-coated duct systems; use actual station/duct construction rather than decorative exposed fans.
- Epiroc Serpent technical specification: https://www.epiroc.com/content/dam/epiroc/underground-mining-and-tunneling/infrastructure/infrastructure-technical-specifications/9869_0012_01b_Serpent_ventilation_technical_specification_english.pdf — manufacturer reference for further dimensional and image inspection.
- Caterpillar AD45: https://www.cat.com/en_US/products/new/equipment/underground-hard-rock/underground-mining-trucks/104983.html — underground articulated truck, ground-level service access, protected wiring and serviceable filters. Current generic tall truck is not an accurate AD45 model.
- WorkSafe NZ ventilation code: https://www.worksafe.govt.nz/dmsdocument/140-acop-ventilation-in-underground-mines-and-tunnels/ — reference explains fresh-air placement, traffic clearance, silencers and recirculation prevention. Not an Australian compliance determination.

## Required visual corrections / unresolved decisions

- User's factory containing a mine portal is an illustrative composite, not yet supported as a reproduction of a specific operating site. Preserve the requested concept while labelling its status honestly.
- Fan intake must have a credible fresh-air route; do not imply workshop exhaust is suitable mine intake air. Show a dedicated service/intake zone and avoid obstructing traffic.
- Add credible fan guards, silencer housings, duct suspension and supports; inspect manufacturer imagery before final geometry.
- Match truck type to context. A surface haul truck can occupy a surface maintenance bay, but should not be depicted entering an undersized underground portal. An AD45-style articulated underground vehicle is a different silhouette and configuration.
- Current tunnel, truck and workshops remain prototypes. Need operator-site imagery, equipment dimensions, protected pedestrian area, service isolation cues and a specialist review before claiming operational authenticity.
